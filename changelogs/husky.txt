ExpressiveOS 1.1.1 for the Pixel 8 Pro — Golden Harvest
==============================

ExpressiveOS 1.1.1 is a small security and stability update to 1.1. It tightens the checks on system features that regular apps should never reach, and fixes a handful of bugs found since 1.1.
On 1.1, it's a smaller update made just for your build: open the Updater and install it, and your apps and data stay. On 1.0, the Updater offers the full update, and your apps and data stay too. Coming from stock Android or another ROM, installing it wipes the phone, so back up first.

───────────────────────

✨ What's New & Improved
==============================

1. 🛡️ Security fixes
- Before: a few system features that ExpressiveOS inherited didn't check carefully enough which app was asking.
- Now: they answer only Android itself and the apps built into ExpressiveOS. App Lock, the private space, Game Space, freeform windows, the lock screen weather and the AirPods pop-up work as before. This update matters for every phone, so install it soon.

2. 🔐 Privacy
- App Data Backup is gone: it saved copies of your apps' data, sign-ins included, as unencrypted archives (unless you set a passphrase) in your phone's storage, where other apps with file access could read them. It's no longer part of ExpressiveOS. If you made backups with it, delete the AppDataBackup folder in your phone's storage.
- Now Playing stays on your phone: with the Dynamic Bar on, a song Now Playing recognized without cover art was looked up on an online music search. Now the Dynamic Bar shows the notification's own artwork, or a music note when there is none, and nothing leaves the phone.

3. 🔒 App Lock hides notifications right away
- Before: after you locked an app, its notifications could keep showing their content until SystemUI restarted.
- Now: a newly locked app's notifications are hidden in the notification shade and on the lock screen as soon as you lock it, including ones already showing.

4. 🧭 Navigation bar styles work with Expressive Launcher
- Before: Studio's navigation bar styles listed only Default, or changed nothing, because they targeted a launcher that doesn't draw the bar.
- Now: the styles list and apply for Expressive Launcher, which draws your navigation bar. If you had picked a style before, pick it again once after updating.

5. 🔧 Fixes
- Lock screen widgets now follow Bluetooth, flashlight and ringer changes.
- Pulse with vibration on only vibrates while music plays, and stops when the music stops.
- Gaming mode no longer stays on when the phone restarts during a game.
- Studio: the battery bar's low battery colour applies, the wallpaper style options match the style you just picked, Dynamic Island's options show once, and Settings' wallpaper type screen opens its own page again.
- The Updater tells you when a new release is out, shows the new release's own notes (1.1 showed the installed build's), refuses a local update made for a different phone, and never offers an update with an older security patch, which would erase the phone.
- Lock screen notifications with blur on are prepared once instead of twice, so they show up faster.
- Less background work: with the Dynamic Bar off, SystemUI no longer checks the battery time estimate every 2 minutes while you use the phone.

💬 Community Notes
==============================

- Who it's for: the Pixel 8 Pro (husky) only. Install the build made for your exact phone.
- Updating from 1.1 or 1.0: open the Updater and install 1.1.1; your apps and data stay. If you added root yourself, an update replaces the boot images, so add it again afterwards.
- Installing fresh: ExpressiveOS's packages don't carry the bootloader or modem. Flash Google's latest Android 17 factory image for your phone first, then ExpressiveOS, with the web installer or fastboot.
- Found a bug? Post it in the Bugs topic of the Expressive Group on Telegram.
- Validation:
  - This build comes from the same sources as the Pixel 7 Pro build, which was checked on a phone before release.

🧩 Release Riddle
==============================

I guard the door to your locked apps, your private space and your system's settings, and in 1.1.1 I finally learned to ask for ID. What am I?

Answer: A permission check! 1.1.1 adds them where they were missing, so only Android and the apps built into ExpressiveOS get through. 🛡️
