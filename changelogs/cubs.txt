ExpressiveOS 1.2 for the Pixel 11 — Frost Chrysanthemum
==============================

ExpressiveOS 1.2 is the November Pixel Drop release: Google's November software for your phone underneath, newer Google apps, a battery charge limit that stays on, a fix for a font that crashed apps, a clearer Updater, and security and battery fixes.
On 1.1.2, it's a small update made just for your build: open the Updater and install it, and your apps and data stay. On an older ExpressiveOS, the Updater offers the full update, and your apps and data stay too. Coming from stock Android or another ROM, installing it wipes the phone, so back up first.

───────────────────────

✨ What's New & Improved
==============================

1. 🍂 Built on Google's November Pixel Drop
- Before: ExpressiveOS ran on Google's October software for your phone.
- Now: the vendor software, kernel and firmware come from Google's November Pixel Drop image for your phone (CP4A.261105.040.D1), with the November security patch.
- Many of the built-in Google apps are updated to the versions in the November Pixel Drop.

2. 🔋 "Limit to 80%" stays on
- Before: Settings > Battery > Charging optimisation > Limit to 80% turned itself off as soon as you left the page.
- Now: it stays on and turns on the phone's 80% charging limit.
- Thanks to Warren in the Expressive Group for reporting it.

3. 🔤 The Sans-Serif font no longer crashes apps
- Before: picking the Sans-Serif font in Expressive Studio could make apps close as they opened.
- Now: it works like every other font.

4. 🔄 A clearer Updater
- Before: an update you installed from a file stayed in the list afterwards, and a build with an older security patch was refused without a clear reason.
- Now: the list is clean after an update, the Updater says plainly why it won't install an older security patch, and What's new can read an update's notes from the update file itself.
- If you added root yourself, the Updater now reminds you how to keep it before you restart.

5. 🔒 Security and battery fixes
- App Lock: a locked app could still show through the Dynamic Bar, and a locked app's notification text could leak. Both are closed.
- Wi-Fi: the phone scanned for networks every 10 seconds from startup until the screen first turned off. It doesn't any more.
- Holiday themes: if applying a holiday wallpaper fails, your own wallpaper and its backup are kept.

6. 🎧 ViPER4Android
- Before: ViPER4Android was on the Pixel 7 Pro only.
- Now: it's built in on your phone too. Open the ViPER4Android app, turn on the master switch and play something.

💬 Community Notes
==============================

- Who it's for: the Pixel 11 (cubs) only. Install the build made for your exact phone.
- Updating from 1.1.2 or older: open the Updater and install 1.2; your apps and data stay. If you added root yourself, an update replaces the boot images, so add it again afterwards.
- Installing fresh: ExpressiveOS's fastboot packages don't carry the bootloader or modem. Flash Google's November Android 17 factory image for your phone first, then ExpressiveOS, with the web installer or fastboot.
- Found a bug? Post it in the Bugs topic of the Expressive Group on Telegram.
- Validation:
  - This build comes from the same sources as the Pixel 7 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL builds, which were checked on phones before release.
